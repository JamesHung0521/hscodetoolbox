Cookie Policy

Last updated:

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make sites work, improve performance, and (with your consent) provide analytics.

2. Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary: Required for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie choice). These cannot be switched off.
  • Local storage (not cookies): Your recent HS code searches and loading inputs are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not access this data.
  • Analytics: If you consent, we may use tools such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. These may set cookies to count visits and track traffic sources.
  • Advertising: If advertising is enabled on the site (for example Google AdSense), Google may use cookies to serve and measure ads. See Google Advertising Technologies.

3. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect site functionality. To opt out of Google Analytics across all websites, see Google's opt-out page. For Google ads personalization controls, see Google Ads Settings.

4. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

5. Contact

Questions about our cookie use? Contact us.
