Privacy Policy

1. Overview

HS Code Toolbox ("we", "us") operates hscodetoolbox.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep data collection minimal.

2. Information we collect

  • Usage data: Standard web analytics (such as pages visited and approximate location) may be collected to understand how the tools are used and to improve them.
  • Local tool data: Your recent HS code searches and container-loading inputs are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and never transmitted to us.
  • Contact messages: If you contact us, we receive the details you voluntarily provide (name, email, and message).

3. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the tools and content.
  • To respond to your enquiries.
  • To measure site performance via analytics (subject to your cookie choices).

4. Cookies

We use only essential and, where you consent, analytics cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details. We do not use cookies for cross-site advertising or to track you across unrelated websites.

5. Advertising & third parties

If and when we display advertising (such as Google AdSense), third-party ad networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can manage these through your browser settings and the ad network's own controls.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers who help operate this site (for example hosting and analytics), bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

7. Data retention & your rights

We retain contact messages only as long as needed to respond. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you by contacting us.
