About

Tools built from the factory floor

HS Code Toolbox started from a simple frustration: most trade tools are either overloaded with enterprise features, written for shipping agents rather than the people actually packing and shipping the goods, or hidden behind sign-up walls and confusing jargon.

We are trade operators, not a marketing team. This site exists to answer the day-to-day questions that actually come up when you are about to ship: what HS code do I use, which container fits my cargo, and am I about to overload it?

Everything here is free, no sign-up required, and focused on practical, actionable answers. Our guides are written from hands-on exporting experience, and we deliberately prefer real numbers and real scenarios over theoretical fluff.

Our dual engine

Tools + content. Quick calculators and lookups for instant answers, backed by a knowledge base that explains the "why" — so you can make better, more confident shipping decisions.

A note on accuracy

Trade rules, duty rates and codes change. Our tools are provided for reference and should always be cross-checked with your customs broker or the official tariff of the destination country.
